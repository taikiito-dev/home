## Where it started

It started from wanting to explain what I had been doing in words my wife could follow.

Since September 2026 I have been moving the things I had left sitting on Google, Apple and other large services — photos, passwords, files, mail, contacts — onto hardware I pay for. The dated dev diary I kept at the time assumed technical background and was mostly notes to myself, so I rewrote the experience in a form that needs **no prior knowledge**. That is what the decision records under [Writing](/writing/) are for.

## What this site is, and is not

Not general advice, not a best-practices collection: **only what I actually did**, including the detours, the mistakes, and the things I gave up on. Knowing the author got stuck in the same place is, in practice, the more reassuring document.

## I did not type the commands

**The commands and configuration on this site were typed by Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant.** I am not an engineer. What I did was decide what to do, run the result on my own machines, and doubt the all-clear.

At first the writing read as though I had built it all myself. In October 2026 I changed that, because hiding it deletes the most useful part. You, reading this, probably have access to Claude or ChatGPT too — so the steps are available to you on request, newer and fitted to your machine. **What you cannot get on request is where it stalls and what is worth protecting.** Those I decided, so those I can write. So what gets published here is the decisions; the steps are left out.

One page breaks that rule: the deployment guide, which the maintainers of the software asked me to write because nobody else was running it that way. Its commands are not mine either, but every failure it describes is — I hit all of them.

Host names in the articles are replaced with placeholders like `vault.example.com`: writing down which address runs what amounts to publishing a floor plan of my own setup.

## How the site is laid out

- **[Writing](/writing/)** — finished pieces: dated decision records, and one operations guide the MulmoClaude maintainers asked for. Corrected when wrong, dated when changed, never rewritten to look better. Only a few live here.
- **[Projects](/projects/)** — what I have built, and where the source lives.

A dated dev diary used to live here too. It came down in October 2026: most of it was not something I could stand behind in my own words.
